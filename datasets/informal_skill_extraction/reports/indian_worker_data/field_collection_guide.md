# ELEVATE X AI — FIELD WORKER INTERVIEW & DATA COLLECTION GUIDE
## Standard Operating Procedures for Independent Field Enumerators & Interviewers
### Phase 1: 100 Genuine Informal Worker Pilot across Four Trades
*Tailor & Garment Worker (25) | Electrician (25) | Plumber (25) | Motor Mechanic (25)*

---

## 1. MISSION & ETHICAL COMMITMENT

The goal of Elevate X AI is to create an artificial intelligence system capable of recognizing the deep experiential, practical, and tactile competence of informal-sector Indian workers. Most informal craftspeople possess master-level skills acquired through years of apprenticeship (Ustaad-Shagird parampara) rather than formal degrees or English resumes.

As an interviewer, your role is to capture **verbatim natural descriptions** of how workers perform their daily tasks, use their tools, troubleshoot breakdowns, and make independent technical judgments.

### Non-Negotiable Ethics & Data Privacy Rules:
1. **Zero Fabrication**: Never invent worker responses, synthesize interviews, or copy text from online tutorials.
2. **Voluntary Participation**: Participation is 100% voluntary. Never pressure or coerce a worker.
3. **Data Minimization (DPDP Act 2023)**: Never ask for or record Aadhaar numbers, PAN, bank accounts, voter IDs, exact home addresses, or phone numbers.
4. **Anonymization**: Every participant is assigned a pseudonymous alias (e.g., `WRK-DEL-TLR-001`).
5. **Right to Withdraw**: If a worker asks to stop or withdraw their interview at any point, immediately honor the request and delete the record.

---

## 2. HOW TO APPROACH WORKERS RESPECTFULLY

Informal workers are busy artisans whose working hours directly determine their daily livelihood. Always approach them respectfully and be mindful of their workflow:

1. **Optimal Timing**:
   - **Tailors**: Avoid festival seasons (Diwali, Eid, wedding months) during peak afternoon cutting hours. Approach during mid-morning (10:30 AM – 12:00 PM) or early tea breaks.
   - **Electricians**: Catch them at independent supply shops or hardware clusters early morning before they head out to site visits (9:00 AM – 10:30 AM).
   - **Plumbers**: Approach at plumbing sanitary ware hardware stores or during late afternoons between job calls.
   - **Motor Mechanics**: Approach at garage clusters during post-lunch lull periods (2:30 PM – 4:00 PM). Never interrupt during active road-side breakdowns or delicate engine hoist operations.
2. **Introduction Script (Hindi Vernacular)**:
   > *"नमस्ते उस्ताद जी / भैया जी, मेरा नाम [आपका नाम] है। हम भारत के हुनरमंद कारीगरों के काम और हुनर को मान्यता दिलाने के लिए एक शैक्षणिक शोध परियोजना पर काम कर रहे हैं। हम यह समझना चाहते हैं कि आप अपने काम में कौन से औजार इस्तेमाल करते हैं और काम कैसे पूरा करते हैं। क्या आपके पास 10-15 मिनट का समय होगा बातचीत करने के लिए? आपकी पहचान पूरी तरह गुप्त रखी जाएगी।"*
3. **Respecting Workplace Safety**:
   - Never touch running machinery, power looms, open electrical boards, or suspended engine blocks.
   - Never ask an electrician for a "live demonstration" on energized wires.

## 3. HOW TO REQUEST INFORMED CONSENT (DPDP ACT 2023)

Before asking any technical questions, walk the worker through the consent options using the bilingual forms built into the collection console.

### Three Separate Consent Layers:
1. **Layer 1: Text Interview Consent (MANDATORY)**:
   - Covers recording the worker's spoken task descriptions, transcribing verbatim words, and annotating actionable skills.
   - If the worker does not agree to text interview consent, **do not proceed**. Thank them for their time.
2. **Layer 2: Audio Recording Consent (OPTIONAL)**:
   - Ask: *"क्या हम आपकी बातचीत को ऑडियो में रिकॉर्ड कर सकते हैं ताकि हम बाद में आपके शब्द सही से लिख सकें?"*
   - If they agree, check the Audio box. If they prefer not to be voice-recorded, take real-time verbatim notes in the console.
3. **Layer 3: Workplace Demonstration Photos (OPTIONAL)**:
   - Ask: *"क्या हम आपके काम करने वाले औजारों या काम की फोटो ले सकते हैं? आपका चेहरा फोटो में नहीं आएगा।"*
   - Never photograph faces, personal ID documents, or cash registers.

## 4. INTERVIEW TECHNIQUE: OPEN-ENDED VS LEADING QUESTIONS

The objective is to elicit natural domain terminology (e.g. *jhirri kaatna*, *solvent lagana*, *bakram chipkana*, *tappet milana*). Never put words in the worker's mouth.

### Avoiding Leading Questions:

| ❌ Leading / Biased Prompt (DO NOT USE) | ✅ Open-Ended Natural Prompt (USE THIS) |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Do you know how to use a single-needle machine?" | *"Aap dukaan par silai ka kaam kaise shuru karte hain? Kaun si machine chalate hain?"* |
| "Do you test neutral-phase voltage with a multimeter?" | *"Jab kahin light nahi aati, toh aap supply kaise check karte hain? Kaun sa tool lagate hain?"* |
| "Do you apply solvent cement on CPVC pipes?" | *"CPVC pipe ko aapas mein jodte waqt aap kya process follow karte hain?"* |
| "Do you bleed disc brakes using a wrench?" | *"Brake service karte time jab break loose hota hai, tab aap kya check karte hain?"* |

### The 10 Essential Probing Dimensions:
For every task the worker mentions, gently probe these 10 dimensions without interrupting their flow:
1. **Specific Action**: What exact work are you doing right now?
2. **Tools**: Which tools, machines, or instruments are in your hands?
3. **Materials**: Which pipes, wires, fabrics, lubricants, or fasteners are being worked on?
4. **Sequence**: What is the step-by-step process from beginning to end?
5. **Quality Verification**: How do you know the cut, connection, or weld is exact?
6. **Common Problems**: What typically goes wrong (e.g. thread breaking, pipe leaking, engine misfire)?
7. **Diagnostics**: How do you pinpoint where the fault is?
8. **Fix / Repair**: What technique do you use to correct the issue?
9. **Safety**: What precautions do you take to avoid injury or equipment damage?
10. **Artisanal Judgment**: What decisions do you make on your own without asking the customer?

## 5. OCCUPATION-SPECIFIC GUIDING QUESTIONS

### A. Tailor and Garment Worker (Target: 25 Workers)
- *"Grahak aane par measurement kaise lete hain? Inch tape kahan lagate hain?"*
- *"Kapde ki marking aur cutting mein kaun se auzaar lagte hain? Katori blouse ya suit mein dart kaise banate hain?"*
- *"Single-needle machine aur overlock machine mein kya farq hai? Overlock kinari par kyun lagate hain?"*
- *"Collar ya cuff banate waqt bakram (interlining) kaise chipkate hain?"*
- *"Agar kapda silai ke waqt khinch raha ho ya dhaaga tod raha ho, toh machine ki setting kaise theek karte hain?"*

### B. Electrician (Target: 25 Workers)
- *"Naye makan mein concealed wiring karte waqt jhirri kaise kaat te hain aur pipe kaise daalte hain?"*
- *"Distribution board mein MCB aur neutral-earth link ka connection kaise karte hain?"*
- *"Earthing pit kaise banate hain? Usme namak, koyla ya chemical kaise daalte hain?"*
- *"Short circuit hone par ya MCB bar-bar trip hone par fault kaise dhoondhte hain?"*
- *"Multimeter se phase aur neutral ki voltage kaise measure karte hain?"*

### C. Plumber (Target: 25 Workers)
- *"CPVC aur UPVC pipe fitting mein solvent cement lagane ka kya niyam hai?"*
- *"GI pipe par chudi (threads) kaatne ke liye die kaise chalate hain aur teflon tape kaise lagate hain?"*
- *"Bathroom mein wall diverter ya concealed mixer install karte waqt level kaise milaate hain?"*
- *"Underground ya deewar ke andar pipe leak ho toh kaise pata lagate hain?"*
- *"Overhead tank fitting mein air pipe, union aur valve ka connection kaise karte hain?"*

### D. Motor Mechanic (Target: 25 Workers)
- *"Bike ya car service ke waqt engine oil kab aur kaise badalte hain? Purana oil kaise drain karte hain?"*
- *"Disc brake aur drum brake mein kya farq hai? Brake oil bleed kaise karte hain?"*
- *"Clutch tight ya loose hone par plate aur cable ki setting kaise karte hain?"*
- *"Battery dead ho ya charge na le rahi ho, toh terminal voltage kaise test karte hain?"*
- *"Carburetor ya fuel line mein kachra aane par use kaise khol kar clean karte hain?"*

## 6. MULTILINGUAL INPUT & SCRIPT GUIDELINES

1. **Devanagari Hindi**:
   - If transcribing in Devanagari, maintain spoken colloquial Hindi without formal Sanskritized editing:
   - Example: *"हम सीपीवीसी पाइप कटर से काट कर सॉल्वेंट लगाते हैं और 15 मिनट सूखने देते हैं।"*
2. **Roman Hindi / Hinglish**:
   - If transcribing in Latin script, use natural phonetic spelling matching how workers speak in WhatsApp or field speech:
   - Example: *"Main 2 saal se dukaan par bike ka engine oil change karta hu aur feeler gauge se plug gap set karta hu."*
3. **Preserve Code-Switching**:
   - Retain technical English loanwords mixed into Hindi (*multimeter, solvent, MCB, overlock, caliper, clutch plate*). Never force-translate *multimeter* to *बहुमापी यंत्र*.

## 7. OPERATING THE LOCAL COLLECTION DASHBOARD

1. **Launch the Station**:
   ```bash
   backend/.venv/bin/python datasets/informal_skill_extraction/scripts/indian_worker_data/launch_dashboard.py
   ```
2. **Open Browser**: Navigate to `http://localhost:8501`.
3. **Start Interview**:
   - Click **New Interview** tab.
   - Select Trade (`TRD-01`, `TRD-02`, `TRD-03`, `TRD-04`).
   - The worker pseudonym ID (e.g., `WRK-DEL-TLR-042`) is auto-generated.
   - Enter years of experience, work context, and primary language.
   - Review the consent form with the worker and tick the **Text Consent** checkbox.
   - Enter Task #1 description.
   - If the worker describes another operation, click **+ Add Another Task** to capture Task #2 and Task #3.
4. **Draft vs Submit**:
   - If the worker needs to attend to a customer or pause, click **Save Draft**.
   - When the interview is finished, click **Validate & Submit Interview**.
   - The console runs instant validation: verifies consent, non-empty description, schema conformance, and appends to `raw/indian_worker_data/real/pilot_{trade}_batch1.jsonl`.
5. **Interactive Skill Annotation**:
   - Go to **Records & Annotator** tab.
   - Click **Annotate** on the submitted record.
   - Simply highlight any skill phrase with your mouse. The console automatically populates the exact character offsets!
   - Select the skill category and canonical skill name.
   - Click **Add Tagged Span** and **Save Annotations**.
