## Guide for Field Interviewers: How to Probe for Depth

To prevent one-line, superficial descriptions (e.g. *"Main silai karta hu"*), interviewers must administer these **ten-probe depth expansions** across 3–5 distinct tasks for each worker:

1. **What**: Exact physical task or operation executed.
2. **Why**: Purpose and intended functional outcome.
3. **Tools**: Hand tools, power equipment, and measuring gauges utilized.
4. **Materials**: Substrates, chemicals, consumables, and raw material grades.
5. **Sequence**: Step-by-step procedural workflow from setup to completion.
6. **Failure Modes**: What typically goes wrong (material tearing, motor stalling, leakage).
7. **Diagnostics**: How the worker detects the defect using sensory or instrument cues.
8. **Rectification**: How the worker rectifies the fault or adjusts machine calibration.
9. **Quality Check**: How the worker inspects and verifies tolerances before delivery.
10. **Safety**: Protective precautions, PPE, and hazard mitigation.

## 1. Tailor and Garment Worker (`TRD-01`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Darzi, Masterji, Karigar, Sewing Machine Operator*
- **Qualification Pack**: `AMH/Q1947` (Level 4) | **O*NET**: `51-6052.00` | **ESCO**: `ff49bb55`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Body Measurement**: Taking torso, chest, inseam, sleeve, and neck measurements using flexible tailor's tape; noting postural asymmetries (sloping shoulders, stoop).
2. **Fabric Identification & Grain Alignment**: Identifying silk, chiffon, cotton, denim, or blended knits; laying fabric along warp/weft grain to prevent bias distortion.
3. **Drafting & Pattern Marking**: Drawing cutting layout chalk lines directly on fabric using tailor's chalk (*kedia/mitti*); adding seam allowances.
4. **Precision Cutting**: Sizing nested pattern components with high-carbon tailoring shears without snagging delicate fibers.
5. **Fusible Canvas / Interlining (Bakram)**: Iron-pressing heat-activated fusible stiffener onto collars, lapels, and cuffs.
6. **Lockstitch Assembly**: Operating industrial single-needle lockstitch machines; controlling knee lifter, foot pedal throttle, and fabric feeding.
7. **Thread Tension Calibration**: Balancing upper thread tension discs and bobbin case tension screw to avoid skipped or looped stitches.
8. **Collar & Cuff Construction**: Turning crisp points, top-stitching, and setting collar bands into the neckline.
9. **Sleeve Insertion & Armhole Setting**: Easing sleeve crowns into armholes without unwanted puckering or gathers.
10. **Overlock Seam Edging**: Trimming raw edges and looping 3-thread or 5-thread overlock chains to prevent seam fraying.
11. **Trouser Construction & Fly Insertion**: Stitching J-stitch zipper fly, side pockets, waistband, and crotch curve.
12. **Buttonholing & Button Attachment**: Cutting bound buttonholes or operating buttonhole attachment; securing horn/acrylic buttons.
13. **Customer Trial & Fitting**: Pinning fit corrections during live trial; assessing chest drape, collar roll, and hem drop.
14. **Alteration & Refitting**: Opening side seams with seam rippers, taking in waistlines, shortening trousers, and resetting shoulder slopes.
15. **Preventive Machine Maintenance**: Oiling needle bar, shuttle race, cleaning lint from feed dogs, and replacing bent or burred needles (size 11 to 18).

## 2. Electrician (`TRD-02`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Bijli Mistri, Wireman, Line Karigar, Electrical Technician*
- **Qualification Pack**: `ELE/Q3104` (Level 4) | **O*NET**: `47-2111.00` | **ESCO**: `6803c737`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Concealed Conduit Chasing**: Cutting parallel grooves in brick masonry with angle grinders; chipping channels with cold chisels to embed PVC pipes.
2. **Conduit Pipe Jointing & Solvent**: Bending rigid PVC conduits with bending springs; solvent-bonding elbow bends and junction boxes.
3. **Cable Pulling & Wire Grouping**: Pulling color-coded copper multi-strand conductors (Red-Yellow-Blue phase, Black neutral, Green earth) through conduits with steel fish tape.
4. **Distribution Board (DB) Dressing**: Terminating incoming mains into double-pole MCBs, residual current circuit breakers (RCCB/ELCB), and busbars.
5. **Circuit Segregation & Load Balancing**: Splitting lighting circuits (1.5 sq mm) from power socket circuits (2.5 & 4 sq mm); allocating AC/geyser loads.
6. **Switchboard & Modular Box Fitting**: Stripping wire ends, connecting modular switches, 2-pin/3-pin sockets, and fan regulators with clean terminal looping.
7. **Earthing Pit Installation**: Digging 3-meter earth pit, positioning GI or copper earth plate, backfilling with charcoal and salt layers, and measuring resistance.
8. **Continuity & Short-Circuit Testing**: Testing circuits with digital multimeters in continuity buzzer mode to identify phase-to-neutral or phase-to-ground faults.
9. **Voltage & Phase Diagnostic**: Measuring 230V AC phase-to-neutral and phase-to-earth voltages; detecting floating neutral conditions.
10. **Inverter & Tubular Battery Setup**: Wiring bypass changeover switches, connecting DC battery cables with petroleum jelly, and checking charging current.
11. **Ceiling Fan & Appliance Mounting**: Securing ceiling hooks, testing capacitor health (2.5 $\mu$F), and verifying clockwise blade rotation.
12. **Preventive Electrical Safety**: Implementing lock-out tag-out (LOTO), using insulated combination pliers (1000V rated), and testing de-energization with phase tester screwdrivers.

## 3. Plumber (`TRD-03`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Nalka Mistri, Plumbing Mistri, Pipe Fitter, Sanitary Karigar*
- **Qualification Pack**: `PSC/Q0104` (Level 4) | **O*NET**: `47-2152.00` | **ESCO**: `aa323101`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Pipe Sizing & Cutting**: Measuring pipe runs; cleanly cutting CPVC, UPVC, and PPR pipes with ratchet pipe cutters; chamfering burrs.
2. **Solvent Cement Jointing**: Applying CPVC/UPVC primer and heavy-duty solvent cement; executing quick 90-degree twist joints and holding for set time.
3. **GI Pipe Threading**: Clamping galvanized iron pipe in pipe vice; cutting external NPT/BSPT threads with manual die heads using cutting oil.
4. **Thread Sealing**: Wrapping PTFE teflon tape clockwise on threaded male nipples; applying thread sealant paste to prevent high-pressure weeping.
5. **Concealed Diverter Installation**: Aligning hot and cold water inlet levels for thermostatic shower diverters inside shower recesses before tiling.
6. **Sanitary Ware Mounting**: Wall-mounting ceramic washbasins and commodes using anchor bolts; leveling with spirit levels.
7. **Flush Cistern Overhaul**: Diagnosing continuous water running in toilet cisterns; replacing deteriorated rubber diaphragm washer or float valve arm.
8. **Overhead Water Tank Setup**: Fitting tank nipples, ball valves, non-return check valves, air release vent pipes, and overflow drains.
9. **Drainage Pipe Slope & Solvent**: Laying 75mm/110mm SWR PVC drainage pipes with minimum 1:50 gravity slope; cementing rubber ring joints.
10. **Nahani Trap & P-Trap Installation**: Embedding water-seal floor traps in cement mortar to block sewer gases and backflow odours.
11. **Water Pump Installation & Air Bleeding**: Connecting domestic centrifugal or submersible pump; priming suction line and bleeding entrapped air pockets.
12. **Hydrostatic Pressure Testing**: Pressurizing completed pipe manifolds with hand pressure test pumps (10–15 bar) to verify joint integrity before plastering.

## 4. Motor Mechanic (`TRD-04`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Auto Mistri, Gadi Mechanic, Two-Wheeler Karigar, Four-Wheeler Karigar*
- **Qualification Pack**: `ASC/Q1402` (Level 4) | **O*NET**: `49-3023.00` | **ESCO**: `85b00c3b`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Engine Oil Drainage & Flushing**: Draining hot sump oil; inspecting oil color and metal flakes; replacing copper crush washers and spin-on oil filters.
2. **Carburetor Cleaning & Jet Tuning**: Disassembling carburetor float bowl; cleaning pilot and main jets with solvent; calibrating air-fuel pilot mixture screw.
3. **Tappet / Valve Clearance Calibration**: Rotating crankshaft to Top Dead Center (TDC); measuring valve clearance using feeler gauges; adjusting rocker arm locknuts.
4. **Clutch Assembly Replacement**: Removing crankcase cover, disassembling clutch springs and pressure plate; inspecting friction plate wear; re-torquing hub nut.
5. **Spark Plug Cleaning & Gap Setting**: Removing carbon fouling with wire brush; calibrating electrode gap (0.7–0.9 mm) using wire feeler gauge.
6. **Hydraulic Disc Brake Bleeding**: Connecting clear vinyl hose to caliper bleed nipple; pumping master cylinder; flushing aerated brake fluid (DOT 4) without bubbles.
7. **Brake Shoe & Drum Servicing**: De-glazing rear drum brake shoes with coarse sandpaper; cleaning dust with brake cleaner; lubricating cam pivot.
8. **Telescopic Front Fork Overhaul**: Disassembling front shock absorbers, draining contaminated fork oil, replacing blown rubber oil seals, and refilling exact fork fluid volume.
9. **Drive Chain Tensioning & Alignment**: Measuring chain slack (25–35 mm); adjusting axle alignment bolts; checking sprocket tooth hooking; lubricating with chain wax.
10. **Battery Specific Gravity & Charging**: Testing lead-acid battery electrolyte specific gravity with hydrometer; cleaning sulphated terminal posts; testing load voltage.
11. **Fault Diagnosis from Engine Symptoms**: Differentiating between fuel starvation, ignition misfire, compression loss, and piston ring blow-by.
12. **Preventive Shop Safety**: Using jack stands under elevated vehicles; disposing hazardous waste oil in designated recovery drums; wearing heat-resistant gloves.

## 5. Carpenter (`TRD-05`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Badhai, Suthar, Khatee, Woodwork Karigar*
- **Qualification Pack**: `FFS/Q0103` (Level 4) | **O*NET**: `47-2031.00` | **ESCO**: `19904ea8`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Plywood Panel Sizing**: Marking cut lines on commercial MR/BWP plywood with try squares; guiding circular saws with straight-edge fences.
2. **Hand Wood Planing (Randa)**: Setting planer iron projection; planing board edges along the wood grain to achieve flat, square reference edges.
3. **Laminate (Sunmica) Pressing**: Spreading synthetic resin adhesive (*Fevicol*) evenly using notched scrapers; degassing air bubbles with wooden rollers.
4. **Mortise and Tenon Joinery**: Marking mortises with marking gauges; chopping square mortise pockets with heavy mortise chisels; sawing snug-fit tenons.
5. **Soft-Close Concealed Hinge Mounting**: Boring 35mm hinge cups using forstner bits; screwing mounting plates to cabinet carcasses; adjusting 3-way alignment screws.
6. **Telescopic Drawer Channel Installation**: Aligning drawer slide rails parallel to within 1mm; ensuring smooth ball-bearing rolling action.
7. **Door Shutter Hanging**: Mortising hinge recesses in door stiles; leveling clearance margins (3mm top/sides, 5mm bottom) using shims.
8. **Decorative Wood Moulding & Beading**: Mitering corner joints at 45 degrees using miter boxes; pinning beadings with brad nails and counter-sinking heads.
9. **Surface Sanding Progression**: Hand sanding timber across progressive grits (80 grit rough, 120 intermediate, 220 fine finish); wiping dust.
10. **Wood Sealer & Polishing Application**: Applying wood filler to nail holes; wiping sanding sealer coats; building French polish (*Lakhdana*) sheens with cotton pads.
11. **Material Estimation & Wastage Minimization**: Calculating optimal sheet nesting layout from cutting lists to minimize plywood and laminate wastage.
12. **Power Tool Safety**: Checking circular saw blade retractable guards; wearing dust respirators when sawing MDF or laminate; wearing ear defenders.

## 6. Welder (`TRD-06`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Welding Mistri, Fabrication Karigar, Welder*
- **Qualification Pack**: `CSC/Q0204` (Level 3) | **O*NET**: `51-4121.00` | **ESCO**: `5903b44b`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Joint Edge Preparation**: Grinding square edges of MS plates into single-V or double-V bevels (60-degree included angle) with 4-inch angle grinders.
2. **Tack Welding (Taka Marna)**: Positioning parts with magnetic squares and clamps; depositing short tack welds to maintain root gaps and prevent heat warpage.
3. **Electrode Selection & Current Calibration**: Selecting AWS E6013 rutile electrodes for light fabrication or E7018 low-hydrogen electrodes for structural joints; setting welding amperage (80–140A).
4. **Arc Striking & Arc Length Control**: Striking electric arc via scratch or tap method; holding 2–3mm arc length without electrode freezing or excessive spatter.
5. **Bead Weaving & Travel Speed**: Depositing stringer and weave beads; balancing travel speed to achieve uniform convex bead reinforcement without undercut.
6. **Slag Chipping & Cleaning**: Chipping glassy slag layer between weld passes using chipping hammer and wire scratch brush; ensuring clean bare metal.
7. **Multi-Pass Welding**: Depositing root pass, hot pass, and capping passes on thick steel joints to achieve full penetration.
8. **Oxy-Acetylene Flame Balancing**: Lighting oxy-fuel torch, setting neutral flame (equal oxygen and acetylene); adjusting preheating inner cones.
9. **Thermal Gas Cutting**: Preheating steel edge to cherry red (850°C); depressing high-pressure oxygen cutting lever to burn through steel with steady drag line.
10. **Weld Defect Rectification**: Grinding out porosity, slag inclusions, or cold-lap cracks; re-welding defective areas.
11. **Grinding & Weld Finishing**: Smoothing structural weld crowns flush with parent metal using flap discs for clean painted surfaces.
12. **Welding Safety & PPE**: Wearing shade 10–11 welding helmet filter lenses, heavy leather gauntlet gloves, leather aprons, and leather spats.

## 7. Mason / Raj Mistri (`TRD-07`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Raj Mistri, Chunai Mistri, Mason Karigar*
- **Qualification Pack**: `CON/Q0103` (Level 4) | **O*NET**: `47-2061.00` | **ESCO**: `572c2ff0`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Mortar Batching & Mixing**: Proportioning cement-to-sand ratios (1:4 for 4.5-inch partition walls, 1:6 for 9-inch load-bearing masonry); adding controlled water.
2. **Brick Soaking & Preparation**: Soaking porous clay bricks in water tanks to saturation point to prevent mortar moisture suction.
3. **Bricklaying Bonds (Chunai)**: Laying staggered English or Flemish bond brick courses with uniform 10–12mm bed mortar joints.
4. **Plumb Bob (Sahul) Alignment**: Checking vertical wall true-ness with brass plumb bobs; maintaining consistent 1–2mm gap between string and brick edge.
5. **Horizontal Stringline (Sutra) Leveling**: Stretcher string lines pulled tight between corner leads to maintain dead-level horizontal courses.
6. **Clear Water Tube Leveling**: Transferring horizontal datum levels across distant walls and lintels using water-filled transparent PVC hose.
7. **Wall Plastering Preparation**: Wetting masonry surface, hacking smooth concrete columns (*tancha maarna*) to ensure mechanical key, and applying cement slurry (*ghol*).
8. **Plaster Screed Level Pads (Bunta)**: Setting plumb plaster mortar dots on walls to guide straight-edge aluminum screeding bars (*fanti*).
9. **Cement Plaster Application**: Troweling cement-sand plaster (12–15mm); leveling with aluminum straight edge; finishing smooth with wooden float (*gurmala*).
10. **Neat Cement Finishing (Ghoontai)**: Troweling pure cement slurry onto wet plaster with steel trowels to create impervious, marble-smooth water-resistant surfaces.
11. **Floor Screed & Tile Setting**: Spreading dry-pack cement-sand bedding with proper drainage slope; combing thinset adhesive with notched trowels; setting ceramic/vitrified tiles with spacers.
12. **Curing Management & Site Safety**: Spraying cured masonry and plaster with water twice daily for 7–14 days; checking scaffolding stability.

## 8. Painter (`TRD-08`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Rang Mistri, Putty Karigar, Painter, Polish Karigar*
- **Qualification Pack**: `CON/Q0501` (Level 3) | **O*NET**: `47-2141.00` | **ESCO**: `ee037596`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Substrate Preparation & Scraping**: Scraping loose flaking paint, efflorescence salts, and grease with stiff metal putty knives and wire brushes.
2. **Wall Putty Mixing**: Hydrating white-cement or acrylic wall putty powder with clean water to a smooth, lump-free paste.
3. **Double-Coat Putty Application**: Skimming first coat vertically with 8-inch steel putty blades; applying second coat horizontally after drying to achieve zero trowel lines.
4. **Progressive Sanding (Regmar)**: Hand-sanding dried putty surfaces with 120-grit paper for initial flattening, followed by 220-grit for mirror smoothness; dusting thoroughly.
5. **Primer Sealing Coat**: Diluting water-thinnable wall primer; rolling onto porous putty substrates to seal porosity and equalize suction.
6. **Waterproofing & Damp-Proof Barrier**: Applying acrylic waterproof coatings (*Doctor Fixit / DampSheen*) onto masonry affected by capillary rising damp.
7. **Paint Thinning & Viscosity**: Diluting premium acrylic interior/exterior emulsions with exact manufacturer-specified water percentages (30–40%).
8. **Roller Painting Technique**: Loading 9-inch microfiber paint rollers evenly in roller trays; applying paint in overlapping 'W' or 'M' patterns to eliminate lap marks.
9. **Cutting-In Brushwork**: Painting sharp, razor-clean boundary edges around ceilings, baseboards, switchboards, and door frames with 2-inch angled sash brushes.
10. **Exterior Weathercoat Painting**: Applying UV-resistant exterior silicone/acrylic emulsion coats onto exterior facades.
11. **Wood Polish & Lacquer Application**: Rubbing sanding sealer into raw wood grains; applying spirit-based French polish with cotton daubers.
12. **Occupational Safety**: Working securely on stepladders using 3-point contact; wearing dust respirators during dry putty sanding; masking floors with drop cloths.

## 10. Mobile Repair Technician (`TRD-10`)
- **Indian Designations**: *Mobile Mistri, Hardware Karigar, Phone Technician*
- **Qualification Pack**: `ELE/Q8104` (Level 4) | **O*NET**: `49-2022.00` | **ESCO**: `71cf906c`

### Granular Task Probes:
1. **Thermal Disassembly**: Heating smartphone back panels on 80°C hotplates; slicing waterproof adhesive with suction clamps and thin plastic pry cards.
2. **Display Folder Separation**: Heating cracked LCD/OLED screens; threading 0.08mm molybdenum cutting wire between broken glass and polarizer film.
3. **OCA Glue Lamination**: Applying Optical Clear Adhesive (OCA) sheets onto replacement front glass; laminating display panels inside vacuum pressure autoclave chambers.
4. **SMD Hot Air Desoldering**: Setting hot air rework station temperature (320°C–360°C) and airflow; applying tacky rosin flux; desoldering burnt charging connectors without melting plastic mic components.
5. **Charging Port (CC Pin) Micro-Soldering**: Tinning micro-USB or Type-C connector solder pads; positioning connector under stereo microscopes; soldering anchor legs and data pins.
6. **DC Benchtop Diagnostic Current Analysis**: Connecting motherboard to regulated DC power supplies (4.2V); reading standby and boot current draw to diagnose dead boards vs display faults.
7. **Short-Circuit Detection (Rosin & Freeze Spray)**: Coating motherboard components with vaporized rosin flux; applying low-voltage DC power to identify overheating capacitor shorts.
8. **Micro-Wire Jumper Circuit Bypass**: Scraping solder mask on broken copper PCB traces under microscopes; soldering 0.02mm enameled jumper wire; curing with UV solder mask resin.
9. **Battery Testing & Thermal Runaway Mitigation**: Testing lithium-ion battery voltage and internal resistance with multimeters; safely extracting swelling batteries with isopropyl alcohol.
10. **BGA Reballing (IC Repair)**: Removing underfill resin; cleaning PCB pads with solder wick; aligning BGA stencils over power ICs and melting solder paste into ball grids.
11. **Post-Repair Quality Testing**: Testing touchscreen digitizer grid sensitivity, proximity sensors, ambient light sensors, microphones, loud speakers, and cellular reception.
12. **ESD Protection & Fume Extraction**: Wearing grounded electrostatic discharge (ESD) wrist straps; using activated carbon soldering fume extractor fans.
