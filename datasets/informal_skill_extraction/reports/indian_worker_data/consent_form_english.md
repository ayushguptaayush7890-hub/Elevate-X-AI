# ELEVATE X AI — WORKER INFORMED CONSENT PROTOCOL (ENGLISH)
## Informed Consent Framework under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023
### Project: Informal Experience to Skill Extraction Benchmark

---

### 1. Purpose of Data Collection
Elevate X AI is an open vocational intelligence and research initiative designed to recognize and validate the tactile, experiential skills of informal trade workers in India (e.g., tailors, electricians, plumbers, mechanics, carpenters). The objective is to translate verbal work narratives in Hindi, Hinglish, and regional dialects into recognized National (NSQF) and International (ESCO, O*NET) skill competency standards.

### 2. Nature of Data & Permitted Processing
- You will be interviewed regarding your vocational journey, day-to-day work tasks, tool handling, troubleshooting procedures, and trade terminology.
- With your explicit permission, the interview will be **audio-recorded** and transcribed into verbatim text.
- The resulting text will be annotated with skill spans and utilized to build natural language processing models for trade competency matching.
- Your data will **never** be sold, licensed to debt collectors, or used for surveillance or credit profiling.

### 3. Absolute Anonymization & Data Minimization
- No high-risk identifiers (Aadhaar numbers, PAN, bank account details, exact residential addresses, or phone numbers) will ever be collected or stored in the research dataset.
- Each participant will be assigned an anonymized unique identifier (e.g., `WRK-DEL-TLR-001`).
- Transcripts will be scrubbed of any inadvertent personal references (e.g., family names, specific building addresses).

### 4. Voluntary Participation & Right to Erasure
- Participation is 100% voluntary. You may decline to answer any question or terminate the interview at any moment without penalty or consequence.
- Under the DPDP Act 2023, you retain the **Right to Withdraw Consent** and request permanent deletion of your recorded audio and transcribed records at any point.
