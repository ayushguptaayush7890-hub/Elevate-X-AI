# ELEVATE X AI — INFORMAL WORKER SKILL INTERVIEW QUESTIONNAIRE (ENGLISH)
## Field Data Collection Protocol: Indian Vocational Trade Workers
### Version 1.0 (November 2026) | DPDP Act 2023 Compliant Protocol

> **Interviewer Guidelines**: This questionnaire is designed for structured face-to-face field interviews with informal, uncredentialed vocational trade workers across India. Do NOT force workers to use textbook or formal English. Encourage them to explain workflows naturally in Hindi, Hinglish, or their local vernacular. Audio record the interview with explicit worker consent.

---

### Section A: Background & Work Setting

1. **Trade Domain & Experience Duration**: What is your primary trade or occupation, and how many years have you been working in this field?
2. **Operational Work Setting**: Where do you primarily carry out your work? (e.g., independent shop/micro-workshop, client site/residence, construction cluster, subcontracted under a master contractor, or home-based)?
3. **Apprenticeship & Learning Pathway**: How did you acquire your skills? Did you undergo an informal apprenticeship under a master craftsman (*ustad* / *masterji*), learn through family tradition, or attend an ITI/vocational training institute?
4. **Client Base & Project Types**: What types of clients and orders do you typically handle (individual retail customers, wholesale vendors, boutique designers, or institutional subcontracts)?

---

### Section B: Daily Work Tasks & End-to-End Workflow

5. **Daily Work Inception**: What is the very first task you perform upon opening your workshop or arriving at the job site each morning?
6. **End-to-End Workflow**: Walk us through a complete job from start to finish—from initial client consultation and measurements to final execution, quality inspection, and delivery.
7. **Tools & Machinery Used**: What specific hand tools, power tools, measuring instruments, and machines do you operate on a daily basis?
8. **Materials Handled**: What raw materials, substrates, and components do you regularly work with (e.g., fabrics, copper wire gauges, pipe grades, plywood types, cement-sand ratios)?
9. **Repair & Alteration Jobs**: Aside from fabricating new items or new installations, what kinds of repair, troubleshooting, or alteration jobs do you handle?

---

### Section C: Technical Skills & Tactile Competence

10. **Machine Operation & Calibration**: How do you set up, calibrate, and operate your primary machines (e.g., needle gauge, tension knobs, blade heights, flame ratios, heat settings)?
11. **Measurement & Layout Planning**: How do you take precise body or site measurements and mark out cutting lines on the substrate before fabrication?
12. **Tactile Craftsmanship & Seam/Edge Finishing**: How do you execute fine craft details, edging, seam sealing, joints, or surface finishing?
13. **Quality Inspection & Tolerance Verification**: Before handing over the job to the client, how do you verify that dimensions, fit, tolerances, and aesthetics meet quality standards?
14. **Procedural Troubleshooting**: When machine issues or material defects arise during execution (e.g., thread breakage, skipped stitches, motor stalling, pipe leaks), how do you diagnose and rectify the fault?

---

### Section D: Complex Jobs & Trade Mastery

15. **Most Challenging Project**: Describe the most difficult, intricate, or complex job you successfully completed in the past year. What made it challenging, and how did you resolve it?
16. **Autonomous Problem Solving**: When encountering non-standard specifications, irregular architectural geometry, or missing parts, what improvisation or trade techniques do you employ?
17. **Apprentice Mentorship**: Do you train helpers, apprentices, or junior workers in your workshop? What is the fundamental skill you teach them first?

---

### Section E: Trade Slang & Vernacular Terminology

18. **Vernacular Tool Names**: What colloquial names do you and your peers use for your tools (e.g., *randa*, *sahul*, *jhirri*, *bakram*, *piko*, *tasla*, *karni*, *fevicol*)?
19. **Trade Slang for Operations**: What terms do you use for specialized operations or techniques (e.g., *tanka marna*, *ghoontai karna*, *tappet set karna*, *regmar maarna*, *folder chipkana*)?
20. **Common Trade Loanwords**: What English loanwords are routinely integrated into your spoken Hindi/vernacular (e.g., *overlock*, *multimeter*, *solvent*, *diverter*, *clutch plate*, *conduit*, *jumper*)?

---

### Section F: Safety, Routine Maintenance & Emergency Care

21. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: What protective gear and physical safety precautions do you follow on the job (e.g., safety glasses, gloves, welding face shield, earthing checks)?
22. **Preventive Machine Maintenance**: How and when do you perform routine cleaning, oiling, lubrication, and consumable replacements on your equipment?
23. **Emergency Response**: If an occupational incident occurs (e.g., needle puncture, electric shock, chemical splash, welding eye arc flash), what immediate first-aid or emergency measures do you execute?
24. **Material Storage & Preservation**: How do you store materials, chemicals, and finished goods to protect them from moisture, corrosion, dust, and physical degradation?
25. **Technology Adaptability**: What modern or automated tools (e.g., electronic lockstitch machines, digital laser levels, computerized diagnostic scanners) are you interested in adopting?
